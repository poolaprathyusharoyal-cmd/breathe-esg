# SOURCES

Research Summary:

1. SAP Fuel and Procurement Data
- SAP systems commonly export data as CSV, Excel, or flat files.
- Sample fuel records were created using source, category, amount, unit, and status fields.
- The prototype simulates enterprise fuel emission uploads.

2. Utility Electricity Data
- Utility teams usually export electricity usage data from portals in CSV format.
- Sample electricity records were created with kWh units.
- Billing and meter complexities were simplified for the prototype.

3. Corporate Travel Data
- Travel platforms like Concur and Navan provide travel activity exports.
- Sample travel data was created for flights and transport activities.
- Distance and emission factor calculations were simplified.

Limitations:
- Real enterprise APIs and integrations were not implemented.
- The project focuses on ingestion workflow and analyst dashboard prototype functionality.
